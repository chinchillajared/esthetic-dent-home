What is included in a Smile Makeover in Costa Rica?

Your Smile Makeover package includes your clinical evaluation, diagnostic imaging, Digital Smile Design, aesthetic and functional treatment planning, custom-made restorations according to the package selected, provisional restorations when needed, final placement and adjustments, a custom night guard, treatment-related follow-up care, bilingual concierge support, domestic airfare between San José (SJO) and Cóbano, and private treatment-related transportation during your stay.

International airfare, accommodations, IV sedation, and additional procedures not included in your written treatment plan are quoted separately.

What treatments can be included in a Smile Makeover?

A Smile Makeover may involve a personalized combination of porcelain veneers, dental crowns, bridges, dental implants, whitening, or other cosmetic and restorative dental treatments, depending on your oral health, bite, existing teeth, and aesthetic goals.

Your dental team will determine which treatments are clinically appropriate for your individual case.

How do I know if I am a candidate for a Smile Makeover?

You may be a candidate if you would like to improve stained or discolored teeth, worn or chipped teeth, uneven tooth shape or size, gaps, asymmetry, or multiple aesthetic concerns affecting your smile.

A clinical evaluation is necessary to determine the appropriate treatment and whether any oral health conditions should be addressed before beginning your Smile Makeover.

How long does a Smile Makeover take?

Most Smile Makeovers in Costa Rica can be completed in a single 5–8 day visit, depending on your individual treatment plan and clinical needs.

Our on-site dental laboratory allows us to coordinate the design, fabrication, evaluation, and necessary adjustments of your restorations during your stay.

Your personalized treatment schedule will be provided before you make your travel arrangements.

Can I see what my smile could look like before treatment?

Yes. During the initial consultation process, digital visualization technology can use a photograph of your smile to provide a glimpse of a potential smile transformation.

This initial visualization is intended to help you explore aesthetic possibilities and communicate your preferences to our dental team. It is for visualization purposes only and is not a clinical treatment plan, prediction, or guarantee of your final results.

Once your clinical evaluation is completed, your actual treatment is developed through personalized clinical planning and Digital Smile Design.

Can I review my Smile Makeover design before my final restorations are made?

Yes. Your Smile Makeover is digitally planned according to your facial features, oral health, bite, clinical needs, and aesthetic goals.

You can review your personalized smile design and discuss necessary refinements with our dental team before your final restorations are fabricated in our on-site dental laboratory.

Are travel services included in the Smile Makeover package?

Yes. Your package includes round-trip domestic airfare between Juan Santamaría International Airport (SJO) and Cóbano, as well as private transportation between Cóbano Airport, your hotel, and the clinic throughout your treatment.

This includes airport pickup upon arrival, transportation to and from your scheduled dental appointments, and transportation back to Cóbano Airport for your departure.

International airfare and accommodations are not included.
