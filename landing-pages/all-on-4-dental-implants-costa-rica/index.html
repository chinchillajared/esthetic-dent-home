All-on-4 Dental Implants: Frequently Asked Questions

How much do All-on-4 dental implants cost in Costa Rica?

The All-on-4 package is $10,480 USD for one arch or $17,775 USD for both the upper and lower arches. All-on-6 and All-on-8 are separate packages with different prices. Your implant specialist will recommend the appropriate option based on your clinical evaluation.

Am I a candidate for All-on-4?

Candidacy depends on your oral health, bone structure, bite, and treatment needs. Your implant specialist will determine whether All-on-4, All-on-6, or All-on-8 is appropriate for you.

Do you offer financing for dental implants?

Yes. Qualified U.S. patients may apply through our independent financing partners. Approval and terms are determined by each provider.

Do dental implants include a lifetime warranty?

Esthetic Dent provides a lifetime implant warranty, subject to eligibility, maintenance requirements, and the written warranty terms.
