Full-Arch Fixed Teeth in Costa Rica

Missing or severely damaged teeth can affect the way you eat, speak, smile, and feel about yourself. Full-arch dental implants in Costa Rica offer a stable, natural-looking solution designed to restore an entire upper arch, lower arch, or both.

Our All-on-4, All-on-6, and All-on-8 dental implant treatments use four, six, or eight strategically positioned implants to support a fixed full-arch restoration. When both the upper and lower arches are treated, this may be described as full-mouth dental implants.

Four, Six or Eight — Decided by Your Anatomy

The appropriate number of implants depends on your bone structure, anatomy, bite, and clinical needs. Your dental team will recommend a personalized treatment plan after reviewing your diagnostic imaging and completing a comprehensive clinical evaluation.

What Are All-on-4?

What Are All-on-4 Dental Implants?

All-on-4 dental implants in Costa Rica provide a fixed full-arch solution for patients who have lost most or all of their teeth—or whose remaining teeth can no longer be predictably restored.

At Esthetic Dent, four strategically positioned dental implants are used to support a fixed prosthesis that replaces an entire upper or lower arch. Implant position and angulation are personalized according to the patient's available bone, anatomy, bite, and restorative needs.

This approach may reduce the need for bone grafting in selected cases. However, the final surgical plan can only be determined after a comprehensive clinical evaluation and 3D CBCT analysis.

Treatment Timeline

What Is the Treatment Timeline for All-on-X Dental Implants in Costa Rica?

Your treatment may be completed in one or two trips to Costa Rica, depending on your bone quality, implant stability, healing requirements, and personalized restorative plan.

01

First Trip: Implant Placement and Fixed Teeth

During your first trip, your dental team completes the required clinical evaluation, diagnostic imaging, surgical planning, and implant placement.

When the implants achieve the stability required for immediate loading, a fixed full-arch restoration may be placed during the same trip. Some patients can complete their planned restoration at this stage, while others receive a fixed provisional restoration designed for the healing period.

02

Healing Period at Home — Approximately 3–6 months

You may be back home, but you are never on your own.

Our care does not end when you leave Costa Rica. Throughout your healing period, our dental team stays connected with you through scheduled virtual follow-ups to monitor your progress, answer questions, and guide you through every stage of recovery.

We will check in with you, review healing updates when needed, and provide personalized instructions for hygiene, eating, and caring for your restoration.

From implant placement to your completed fixed smile, we are with you every step of the way.

03

Second Trip: When Clinically Required

Patients who receive a provisional restoration during the first trip return to Costa Rica after the implants have completed the required healing and integration period.

During this visit, the final restoration is evaluated for fit, appearance, function, comfort, and bite before being securely placed. Necessary refinements are completed before you return home.

The number of trips and treatment schedule vary by patient. Your personalized timeline will be determined after your clinical evaluation and 3D CBCT analysis.

Recovery

What Is the Recovery Process After All-on-X Surgery?

Recovery occurs in several stages over approximately three to six months while the implants integrate with the jawbone. Discomfort, tenderness, bruising, and swelling are common during the first few days and can generally be managed with prescribed medication and personalized post-surgical instructions.

Depending on your clinical plan, you may receive either a fixed provisional restoration during the healing period or your planned fixed restoration during the first trip.

The following table, per the American Academy of Implant Dentistry guidance, explains the four general phases of recovery after All-on-4, All-on-6, or All-on-8 dental implant surgery. Recovery times and experiences vary from patient to patient.

Recovery PhaseTypical TimelineWhat to Expect
Initial Recovery First 24–72 hours Swelling, tenderness, bruising, and minor bleeding may occur. Rest, prescribed medication, cold compresses, and a soft diet are generally recommended.
Early Healing Days 4–14 Discomfort and swelling typically begin to decrease as the gums heal. Your dentist evaluates the surgical sites, bite, hygiene, and fixed restoration.
Implant Integration Weeks 2–12 The implants continue integrating with the jawbone. You may feel increasingly comfortable, but must continue following the recommended diet and hygiene instructions.
Restorative Completion Months 3–6 Implant integration is evaluated. Depending on your treatment plan, your definitive restoration may be placed, adjusted, or clinically reviewed during this phase.

Why All-on-4

Why Can All-on-4 Be So Effective?

All-on-4 uses four strategically positioned implants to support an entire fixed arch. The posterior implants may be angled to maximize the available bone and avoid important anatomical structures.

For qualifying patients, this approach may reduce the need for bone grafting, simplify treatment, and allow a fixed provisional restoration to be placed shortly after surgery. Immediate fixed teeth depend on bone quality, implant stability, and the clinical judgment of your dental team.
