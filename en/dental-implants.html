What Are Dental Implants?

What Are Dental Implants?

A Permanent Solution for Missing Teeth

Dental implants are biocompatible titanium posts placed in the jawbone to replace missing tooth roots. Through a natural process called osseointegration, the implants fuse with the surrounding bone, creating a strong and stable foundation for custom-made zirconia restorations.

Unlike removable dentures, dental implants do not shift, click, or require adhesives. They look, feel, and function more like natural teeth—helping you smile, speak, and eat with confidence.

At Esthetic Dent, your dental implant treatment in Costa Rica is coordinated under one roof—from 3D CBCT imaging and digital treatment planning to the creation of your final restoration in our in-house dental lab.

Who Is a Candidate?

Who Is a Candidate for Dental Implants?

Dental implants may be right for you if you:

  • Are missing one or more teeth and want a fixed, long-term solution
  • Are tired of removable dentures that slip, click, or require adhesives
  • Have a severely damaged or cracked tooth that cannot be saved
  • Have failing crowns or bridges and need a more stable replacement
  • Have enough jawbone to support an implant—or are open to bone grafting when clinically necessary
  • Want to restore your ability to eat, speak, and smile with confidence

Have you been told you do not have enough bone for dental implants? Our implant team can evaluate your case using 3D CBCT imaging and determine whether bone grafting or another personalized treatment option may help you qualify.

How It Works

Your Dental Implant Treatment, From Start to Finish

  1. 01

    Free Virtual Consultation and Personalized Treatment Plan

    Meet online with our dental team for a comprehensive review of your case. We evaluate your dental history, current concerns, photographs, X-rays, and any available 3D scans to understand your clinical needs and treatment goals.

    Based on the information provided, we explain your dental implant options, recommend the most appropriate approach, and prepare a personalized treatment plan with a clearly defined package price. You will know what your proposed treatment includes, how many visits may be required, and what to expect before making any travel arrangements.

    Your journey begins with a real treatment plan—not simply a sales call.

  2. 02

    In-Person Evaluation and 3D Confirmation

    Once you arrive in Costa Rica, we complete your clinical evaluation and 3D CBCT scan to confirm the treatment plan prepared during your virtual consultation. The price of your approved dental implant package remains unchanged for everything included in your plan.

    If an additional procedure outside the package is clinically necessary, it will be clearly explained and quoted separately before you make any decision. If your in-person evaluation shows that you need less treatment than originally planned, we will adjust your treatment and package price accordingly.

  3. 03

    Dental Implant Placement

    A biocompatible titanium implant is precisely placed into the jawbone under local anesthesia. Sedation options are also available when clinically appropriate.

  4. 04

    Healing and Osseointegration

    During a healing period of approximately three to six months, the implant naturally integrates with your jawbone, creating a strong and stable foundation for your final tooth.

  5. 05

    Custom Abutment and Crown

    After the healing period, the abutment is connected to the implant. Our in-house digital dental lab then designs and fabricates your custom zirconia crown.

    Because your clinical and laboratory teams work under one roof, adjustments can be completed efficiently and with greater control over fit, function, and appearance.

  6. 06

    Your Final Result

    Receive a strong, fixed replacement tooth designed to look, feel, and function naturally—backed by our lifetime implant warranty.

No unexpected changes. No hidden fees. No treatment without your approval.

Dental Implants in Costa Rica: Frequently Asked Questions

Dental implant placement is performed under local anesthesia, so you should not feel pain during the procedure. Mild swelling, tenderness, or discomfort for a few days afterward is normal and can usually be managed with the medication prescribed by our dental team. Sedation options may also be available when clinically appropriate.
With proper oral hygiene and regular professional care, dental implants can last for decades—and often a lifetime. That is why we back the implant itself with our lifetime warranty, subject to the warranty's care and maintenance requirements.
We evaluate the quality and volume of your jawbone using 3D CBCT imaging. Depending on your individual case, bone grafting, a sinus lift, or another personalized implant solution may help create the support needed for treatment.
Most dental implant treatments require two visits to Costa Rica. During the first visit, the implant is placed. After approximately three to six months of healing and osseointegration, you return for the placement of your final custom-made zirconia crown.
Flexible financing options may be available depending on your treatment plan and eligibility. Our team can explain the available options and help you determine which one may work best for your needs.
