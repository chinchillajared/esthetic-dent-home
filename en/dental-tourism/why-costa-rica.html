Advanced Care, Natural Recovery

Why Costa Rica for Dental Work

A new smile does not have to come with a stressful recovery.

Costa Rica brings together high-quality dental care, meaningful savings, warm hospitality, and surroundings that invite you to slow down and heal.

Choose the natural beauty of Santa Teresa and Cóbano or the convenience of San José, all just a short flight from the United States.

Talk to a Dental Advisor

Stable & Welcoming

Costa Rica abolished its army in 1948 and has built one of Latin America’s most established democracies. Its warm culture and extensive tourism infrastructure make international visitors feel at home.

Internationally Recognized Care

Costa Rica consistently ranks among Latin America’s leading healthcare and medical tourism destinations. Our dentists pursue training with respected U.S. and international professional organizations, bringing globally recognized techniques and standards to your care.

Closer Than You Think

Nonstop flights connect Costa Rica with several major U.S. cities through its international airports. With minimal time difference, traveling here feels easier, and returning home feels less exhausting.

Choose the Setting That Feels Right

Recover by the Beach or Stay in San José

Your dental recovery in Costa Rica can be as peaceful or as convenient as you need it to be. Choose Santa Teresa or Cóbano for a slower pace near the Pacific coast, where you can rest surrounded by nature between appointments.

Prefer to stay in the city? San José offers convenient airport access, comfortable hotels, restaurants, and everything you may need close by. Wherever you choose, your treatment remains in the hands of the same trusted team.

More Smile, Less Financial Stress

High-Quality Dental Care Without U.S. Prices

Dental work in Costa Rica can cost up to 70% less than comparable treatment in the United States. For patients needing dental implants, crowns, veneers, or full-mouth restoration, those savings can make life-changing care feel possible again.
