Dental Crowns & Bridges FAQs

A dental crown restores and protects a damaged tooth when the root can still be preserved. If a tooth is missing, a dental bridge fills the space using neighboring teeth for support, while a dental implant replaces the missing root and helps preserve the surrounding bone. Our dentist will recommend the right treatment based on your examination, digital scans, and long-term oral health—not simply on price.

Because your crown is digitally designed and milled in our in-house dental lab, treatment can usually be completed within a few days. Many international patients receive their final crown during the same trip as their clinical evaluation, depending on their individual case.

Typically completed within a few days

With proper care, zirconia crowns commonly last 10 to 15 years and may last significantly longer. Their lifespan depends on oral hygiene, bite forces, regular dental checkups, and habits such as teeth grinding. Although the crown protects the tooth, the surrounding gums, bone, and natural tooth structure still require daily brushing and flossing.
