Who Can Benefit

Who May Be a Candidate for a Smile Makeover?

In-House Lab & Technology

Designed Digitally. Created On-Site.

Your Smile Makeover is planned using Digital Smile Design, 3D intraoral scanning, and advanced CAD/CAM technology. Your veneers and crowns are then created in our on-site dental laboratory, where your dentist and dental technician collaborate directly throughout the process.

You will review the proposed shape, size, proportions, shade, and overall appearance of your new smile before your final restorations are fabricated. When clinically appropriate, your dentist may also recommend an in-mouth mock-up to provide an additional physical preview.

With no external laboratory shipping delays, shape, shade, and fit adjustments can be completed on-site, helping us complete your Smile Makeover during the same trip.

How It Works

The Process, Step by Step

01

Free Virtual Consultation

Tell us about your smile goals and explore which package option may be right for you. No X-rays or clinical records are required to get started.

02

Complete In-Person Evaluation

Your dentist completes a comprehensive clinical evaluation, facial and smile analysis, digital scans, and any necessary diagnostic imaging to confirm your final treatment plan.

03

Conservative Tooth Preparation

Each tooth is prepared only as much as clinically necessary for the selected veneer or crown, with an emphasis on preserving healthy tooth structure.

04

Digital Impressions

A 3Shape intraoral scanner captures detailed 3D images of your prepared teeth, which our in-house dental laboratory uses to design your custom restorations.

05

Final Smile Design and Preview

Using your digital impressions, our team creates the final smile design for your review. You can evaluate the proposed shape, size, proportions, shade, and overall appearance before your final restorations are fabricated. When clinically appropriate, your dentist may also recommend an in-mouth mock-up.

06

In-House Laboratory Fabrication — Typically 2–3 Day

Once you approve the final design, our dental technicians fabricate each custom veneer or crown in our on-site laboratory using the material selected for your case.

07

Final Placement and Adjustments

Your final restorations are evaluated for fit, shade, bite, comfort, and overall appearance. Once approved, they are securely placed using the appropriate bonding or cementation technique, followed by final bite and comfort adjustments.
