Porcelain Veneers Costa Rica Package

Your Dental Ceramist Is in the Next Room

Beautiful veneers depend on artistry—not just technology.

Your veneers are digitally designed and finished by hand in our on-site laboratory. Your dentist and ceramist evaluate the shade, translucency, and shape against your real face—not photographs sent to an outside lab.

Adjustments can be made while you are here, allowing every detail to be refined before your veneers are permanently placed.

The Result

The goal is not for people to notice your veneers. It is for them to notice your smile.

Shape, proportion and translucency are matched to your own face, so the work reads as teeth rather than as dentistry.

Common Questions

Veneers FAQ

Our porcelain veneer packages start at $2,995 for a set of six. A single porcelain veneer in the United States can cost $900 or more, making treatment in Costa Rica a significantly more affordable option. Your final cost will depend on the number of veneers required and your personalized treatment plan.
Porcelain veneers typically last 10 to 15 years with proper care, and they may last longer in some cases. Their longevity depends on your oral hygiene, bite, lifestyle habits, routine dental care, and use of the custom night guard provided with your package. Your veneers are protected by our limited lifetime guarantee, subject to the clinic's guarantee terms and conditions.
Most veneer cases can be completed during one trip of approximately five to nine days. Because your veneers are digitally designed and hand-finished in our on-site laboratory, your dentist and ceramist can evaluate and refine their shape, shade, fit, and function while you are here. Your recommended travel schedule will be confirmed after your virtual consultation.
When properly planned and placed, veneers do not actively damage your teeth. We prioritize conservative preparation and preserve as much healthy tooth structure as clinically possible. The amount of preparation required depends on your teeth, bite, alignment, and desired result.
