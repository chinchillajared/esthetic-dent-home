Meet Our Specialists

A multidisciplinary dental team focused on thoughtful planning, clinical precision, and care designed around each patient.

At Esthetic Dent, treatment is planned collaboratively. Our dental professionals bring together experience in oral implantology, endodontics, restorative and aesthetic dentistry, digital workflows, periodontal care, and general dentistry. This team-based approach allows each patient’s case to be evaluated from the perspectives needed to support function, comfort, long-term oral health, and a natural-looking result.

International patients also benefit from clear English-language communication, coordinated appointments, and a team accustomed to organizing comprehensive dental care within a carefully planned visit to Costa Rica.

Experience, Training, and Approach to Care

Dr. Graciela Angulo Orozco

Co-Founder · General & Restorative Dentistry

Co-founder of Esthetic Dent and a trusted dental professional with more than 35 years of experience, whose dedication to patient care helped shape the values and clinical foundation of the practice.

Dr. Graciela Angulo Orozco is a co-founder of Esthetic Dent and one of the professionals whose vision, perseverance, and commitment to dentistry helped establish the practice. With more than 35 years of experience as a dentist, she brings a depth of clinical perspective that can only be developed through decades of caring for patients and following their oral health over time.

See full profile

Dr. Angulo completed her dental education in Cuba, where her professional journey in dentistry began. After bringing that training and experience to Costa Rica, she became a member of the College of Dental Surgeons of Costa Rica in 1978. Throughout her career, she has witnessed the evolution of dentistry across generations — from traditional clinical methods to modern restorative materials, digital planning, and increasingly collaborative approaches to care.

For Dr. Angulo, dentistry has always been about more than treating teeth. It is about listening carefully, earning a patient’s trust, understanding the person behind the clinical need, and providing care with honesty, respect, and compassion. Those principles became part of the foundation on which Esthetic Dent was built and continue to guide the way the team welcomes and cares for every patient.

Today, her experience provides continuity between the history of the practice and its future. As Esthetic Dent continues to grow, incorporate new technology, and welcome patients from Costa Rica and abroad, Dr. Angulo’s legacy remains present in the practice’s commitment to personal attention, responsible care, and long-term patient relationships.

Dr. Fernando Alfaro Víquez

Endodontist

A formally trained endodontist dedicated to preserving natural teeth through advanced diagnosis, root canal treatment, magnification, and modern endodontic technology.

Dr. Fernando Alfaro Víquez completed his dental degree at Universidad Latina de Costa Rica and later returned to the same university for postgraduate specialty training in Endodontics. He also completed a diploma in Endodontics at Pontificia Universidad Javeriana in Colombia, expanding his academic foundation through international study.

See full profile

He has practiced exclusively in Endodontics since 2014 and is registered with the College of Dental Surgeons of Costa Rica as an Endodontics specialist. His work centers on diagnosing dental pain, treating infection inside the tooth, managing anatomically complex root canal systems, and helping patients preserve natural teeth whenever clinically appropriate.

Dr. Alfaro uses contemporary endodontic tools including dental microscopy, rotary instrumentation, electronic apex location, digital radiography, mobile X-ray technology, ultrasonic instrumentation, and thermoplastic obturation. These technologies support visibility, measurement, disinfection, and precision throughout treatment.

His professional development includes training in apical microsurgery and intentional replantation, participation in international endodontic congresses, national lectures, research presentations, and scientific writing. He is also affiliated with the Costa Rican Academy of Endodontics and the Latin American Endodontic Society (SELA).

Areas of Expertise

Great dental care depends on more than one person. Craftsmanship Meets Digital Dentistry

Our Lab Technicians

The people who transform a digital design and a clinical plan into the restorations that complete your smile.

Dental laboratory work is both a science and an art. Behind every carefully designed crown, bridge, veneer, or implant-supported restoration is a technician who understands that precision, function, color, texture, and natural anatomy all matter.

Our laboratory technicians bring experience in their respective areas and work under the clinical guidance of our dentists. Because the laboratory is part of Esthetic Dent’s coordinated workflow, the clinical and technical teams can communicate directly, review details together, and make adjustments without relying entirely on an outside laboratory.

They combine hands-on craftsmanship with advanced digital technology to help create restorations tailored to each patient. Digital scans, design software, modern materials, and precise manufacturing tools support the process, while the technician’s trained eye brings the final shape, proportions, surface character, and natural appearance to life.

The result is a collaborative process in which the dentist defines the clinical requirements and the laboratory team translates that plan into a restoration designed to fit, function, and complement the patient’s smile.

Where Technology Meets Craftsmanship

  • Direct collaboration between the dentist and the dental laboratory
  • Digital workflows for scanning, design, and treatment communication
  • Individualized attention to fit, function, shade, shape, and surface texture
  • Experience working with modern restorative materials and implant-supported solutions
  • Quality review guided by the patient’s clinical plan and aesthetic goals
