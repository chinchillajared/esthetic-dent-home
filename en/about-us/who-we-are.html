Who We Are

Restoring Smiles in Costa Rica for Over 35 Years

When dental problems affect the way you eat, speak, or smile, they affect more than your teeth.

They can affect your confidence, comfort, and quality of life. For over 35 years, Esthetic Dent has helped patients regain those essential parts of their lives through personalized dental care, thoughtful treatment planning, and a team committed to delivering results patients can feel confident about.

Our Team

More Than a Dental Clinic. A Team You Can Trust.

Esthetic Dent brings together dental specialists, laboratory technicians, and patient coordinators under one shared treatment plan.

This collaborative approach allows every member of your care team to understand your needs, follow your progress, and remain involved from your initial evaluation through your final result.

Our in-house dental laboratory and digital technology support this process, giving our clinical and technical teams the ability to work closely together throughout your treatment.

Care Designed Around You

Care Designed Around You

No two patients arrive with the same needs, concerns, or expectations. That is why your care begins with listening.

We take the time to understand what is affecting you, what you would like to change, and what you expect from your treatment. From there, we create a personalized plan and explain every recommendation clearly, so you can make informed decisions about your dental care.

Whether you require a single restoration or a more comprehensive rehabilitation, you receive the same level of attention, communication, and respect.

What Guides Our Care

What Guides Our Care

Our purpose is to help every patient feel informed, supported, and confident throughout their dental care. We combine personalized treatment planning, experienced professionals, modern technology, and clear communication to provide care that considers not only your smile, but also your comfort, health, and long-term needs.

Patient-Centered Care

Every patient arrives with different needs, concerns, and expectations. We take the time to listen, understand your goals, and create a treatment plan designed specifically for you.

Honest and Clear Guidance

We believe you should understand your diagnosis, treatment options, expected timeline, and costs before making a decision. Our team provides clear explanations and recommends treatment based on what is appropriate for your individual case.

Safety and Clinical Responsibility

Your well-being guides every stage of your care. From treatment planning and sterilization protocols to the materials and technology we use, our team approaches each procedure with careful attention and professional responsibility.

Long-Term Commitment

Our relationship with you does not end when your treatment is completed. We provide treatment documentation, follow-up support, and guidance to help you protect and maintain your results over time.
