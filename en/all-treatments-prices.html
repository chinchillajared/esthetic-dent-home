High-quality, affordable dental implants in Costa Rica, performed by specialists.

We now place dental implants from Straumann Group, Zimmer and Nobel Biocare that offer high quality durable materials, long history of research and global availability. Straumann Group dental implants are available upon request.

When teeth extractions are required to place dental implants, these will not be charged to you. Regular non-implant extractions are charged accordingly.

Note: Our implant prices include standard abutments. Customized abutments are recommended and will be charged for specific cases.
