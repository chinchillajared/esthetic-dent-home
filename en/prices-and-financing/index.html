Finance Your Dental Care in Costa Rica

Meet Our Financing Partners

Esthetic Dent partners with Advance Care Card, MyMedicalFunding, and eFinancing Solutions to offer financing options for eligible U.S. patients. Explore all three financing options and choose the one that best fits your credit profile, treatment plan, and budget.

Advance Care Card

Healthcare Financing with Promotional Options

Advance Care Card is designed specifically for healthcare expenses, including dental treatment. Eligible applicants may receive promotional interest-free financing for up to 15 months, depending on their credit profile and approved terms.

  • Free online application
  • Fast credit decision, often instantly
  • Credit limits up to $25,000 or more for highly qualified applicants
  • Co-signers accepted and no prepayment penalties
  • Terms and interest rates based on individual credit approval
Apply with Advance Care Card

MyMedicalFunding

Fixed-Payment Medical Loans

MyMedicalFunding provides installment-loan options for applicants across a range of credit profiles. If you qualify and accept an offer, funds are deposited directly into your bank account for your dental treatment.

  • Secure online application
  • Financing options from $2,000 to $35,000
  • Fast financing decision
  • Fixed monthly payments and interest rate
  • No prepayment penalties
  • Funds deposited directly into your bank account

Checking your options does not affect your credit score; an accepted loan may appear on your credit report.

Explore MyMedicalFunding Options

eFinancing Solutions

Personalized Medical Financing Options

eFinancing Solutions helps eligible patients explore personal loan options for dental treatment through its network of lending partners. Applicants may review personalized offers and select the financing option that best fits their needs and financial situation.

  • Secure online application
  • Access to multiple lending partners
  • Options for a range of credit profiles
  • Initial prequalification with no impact on your credit score
  • Personalized loan offers
  • Rates, fees, and repayment terms based on individual approval
Prequalify with eFinancing Solutions

Common Questions

Pricing FAQ

Why is dental work so much less expensive in Costa Rica?

Lower operating costs, not lower standards. We use the same implant systems and materials as U.S. clinics, and our in-house lab removes outside lab fees.

Are there hidden costs?

No. Your written quote includes the treatment plan discussed with your advisor. If anything changes after your in-person exam, it is explained and approved by you first.

Does the savings really cover the trip?

For most restorative cases, yes, several times over. A single arch restoration can save $10,000 or more, far above typical flight and hotel costs.
